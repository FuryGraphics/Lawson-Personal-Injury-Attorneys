`/public/images` rather than hotlinked so the site carries no runtime dependency on a
third-party CDN.

## The one photograph that is not stock

`yari-lawson.jpg` is the client-supplied headshot of Yari D. Lawson, used on `/attorney` and in
the homepage teaser. It is the only image here of a real person connected to the firm, and it is
the only one that may be used that way — a stock photograph of a stranger standing in for a
named, licensed attorney would be a misrepresentation.

1066×1600, which is comfortably sharp on retina in the 4:5 frames it occupies. Next serves it as
AVIF/WebP at the size actually needed, so the 540 KB source is not what visitors download. It
replaced an earlier 576×653 copy that would have softened on high-density displays.

## What is deliberately *not* an image

No staged accident, injury, or hospital imagery is used anywhere. It reads as exploitative on a
real firm's site, and it is the single most common way injury-firm websites cheapen themselves.
